\section*{Group}

A \textit{group} is a set $G$ with an operation $\cdot$ such that the following properties (often called the \textit{Axioms of Group Theory}) hold:

\begin{itemize}
\item Closure: $\forall a, b \in G, a \cdot b \in G$
\item Associativity: $\forall a, b, c \in G, a \cdot (b \cdot c) = (a \cdot b) \cdot c$
\item Identity: $\exists e \in G$ such that $\forall a \in G, a \cdot e = e \cdot a = a$
\item Inverse: $\forall a \in G, \exists a^{-1}$ such that $a a^{-1} = a^{-1} a = e$
\end{itemize}
