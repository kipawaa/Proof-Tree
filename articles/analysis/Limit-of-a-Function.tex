\subsection{Statement}
A *limit* $\lim_{x \to c} f(x)$ of a \hyperref[articles:analysis:function]{Function} $f(x)$ is equal to a number $L$ iff $\forall \epsilon > 0, \exists \delta > 0$ such that $\lvert x - c \rvert < \delta \implies \lvert f(x) - L \rvert < \epsilon$.
