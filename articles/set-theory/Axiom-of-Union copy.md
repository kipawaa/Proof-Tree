## Statement
For any set $S$ there exists a set $U = \cup S$ such that $x \in U$ iff $x \in A$ for some $A \in S$.
Often, the intersection of two sets $A$ and $B$ is denoted $A \cup B = \\{x \mid x \in A$ or $x \in B\\}$.
