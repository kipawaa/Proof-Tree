## Statement
All [[Natural Numbers]] are [[Transitive Sets|Transitive Set]].

## Explanation

## Proof(s)
Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

If $n = 0$ then $n$ is [[Transitive|Transitive Set]] by vacuousness.

Suppose $n > 0$.\
By definition of [[Natural Numbers]], $n = \{0, 1, 2, \dots, n - 1\}$.\
Hence if $m \in n$ then by the [[Usual Ordering of the Natural Numbers]] we have that $m < n$.\
hence $m \subset n$, as wanted.
