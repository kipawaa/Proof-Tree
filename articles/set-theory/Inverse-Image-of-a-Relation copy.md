## Statement
The *inverse image* of a set $B$ under a [[Relation]] $R$ is a [[Subset]] of the [[Domain of a Relation]] given by $R^{-1}[B] = \\{x \in \textnormal{dom}\\, R \mid \exists y \in B$ for which $(x,y) \in R\\}$.
