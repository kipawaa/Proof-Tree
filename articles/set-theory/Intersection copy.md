## Statement
The *intersection* of a non-empty set $S$ is the set $\bigcap S = \\{ x \mid \forall X \in S, x \in X\\}$.
Often, the intersection of two sets $A$ and $B$ is denoted $A \cap S = \\{x \mid x \in A$ and $x \in B\\}$
