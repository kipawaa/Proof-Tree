## Statement
There is a unique set $C$ such that $x \in C$ iff $x = A$ or $x = B$.

## Explanation

## Proof(s)
By the [[Axiom of Pairing]] there exists a set $C$ such that $x \in C$ iff $x = A$ or $x = B$.\
Suppose there exist two such sets, $C$ and $C'$.\
Notice that $C = \\{A, B\\}$ by definition, and similarly $C' = \\{A, B\\}$ by definition.\
Hence by the [[Axiom of Extensionality]] $C = C'$, as wanted.
