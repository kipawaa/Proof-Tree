## Statement
The [[Natural Numbers]] are [[Well-Ordered|Well-Ordered Set]] by membership, and hence $(\mathbb{N}, \in)$ is an [[Ordinal Number|Natural Numbers]]. This [[Ordinal Number|Ordinal Numbers]] is often denoted $\omega$.
