## Statement
The standard [[Ordering|Partial Ordering]] of the [[Cardinal Numbers]] is given by $\lvert X \rvert \leq \lvert Y \rvert$ iff there exists an [[Injective Function]] $f : X \to Y$.

This induces a strict [[Ordering|Partial Ordering]] of the [[Cardinal Numbers]] given by $\lvert X \rvert < \lvert Y \rvert$ iff $\lvert X \rvert \leq \lvert Y \rvert$ and $\lvert X \rvert \neq \lvert Y \rvert$.
