## Statement
A [[Relation]] is called an *equivalence relation* if it is [[Reflexive|Reflexive Relation]], [[Symmetric|Symmetric Relation]] and [[Transitive|Transitive Relation]].

## Explanation
These are the conditions we require for equality, and we want this preserved when we define similar/new types of equality.
