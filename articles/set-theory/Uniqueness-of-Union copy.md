## Statement
For any set $S$ there exists a *unique* set $U$ such that $x \in U$ iff $x \in A$ for some $A \in S$.

## Explanation

## Proof(s)
By the [[Axiom of Union]] there exists a set $U$ such that $x \in U$ iff $x \in A$ for some $A$ in S$.\
Suppose there exist two such sets, $U$ and $U'$.\
Notice that $U$ and $U'$ are uniquely determined by the elements of subsets of $S$, and hence possess the same elements.\
Hence by [[Axiom of Extensionality]] $U = U'$, as wanted.
