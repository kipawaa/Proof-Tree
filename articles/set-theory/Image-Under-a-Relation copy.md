## Statement
The *image* of a set $A$ under a [[Relation]] $R$ is a [[Subset]] of the [[Range|Range of a Relation]] of $R$ given by $R[A] = \\{y \in \textnormal{ran}\\, R \mid \exists x \in A$ for which $(x,y) \in R\\}$.
