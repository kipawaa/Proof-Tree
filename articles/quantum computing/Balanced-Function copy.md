## Statement
A [[Function]] $f : \\{0, 1\\}^n \to \\{0, 1\\}$ is *balanced* iff $\left\lvert\\{x \in \\{0, 1\\}^n \mid f(x) = 0\\}\right\rvert = \left\lvert\\{x \in \\{0, 1\\}^n \mid f(x) = 1\\}\right\rvert$.
