1.1 Group Theory

Group

A group is a set G with an operation ⋅ such that the following properties (often called the Axioms of Group Theory) hold:

  • Closure: ∀ ⁡a,b ∈ G,a ⋅ b ∈ G
  • Associativity: ∀ ⁡a,b,c ∈ G,a ⋅ (b ⋅ c) = (a ⋅ b) ⋅ c
  • Identity: ∃ ⁡e ∈ G such that ∀ ⁡a ∈ G,a ⋅ e = e ⋅ a = a
  • Inverse: ∀ ⁡a ∈ G,∃ ⁡a−1 such that aa−1 = a−1a = e
